Snug Haus

A cosy little house in the hills of Snug, Tasmania. Views, serenity, simplicity.

“Echidna is back at Snug Haus!”Heidi, October 2026

A timber house for two, in the foothills of the Snug Tiers

Snug Haus looks out over Storm Bay and the D’Entrecasteaux Channel, half an hour from the centre of Hobart.

Your hosts live on the same five-acre block, just up the hill. They’re there if you need them, and there are no other guests.

  • Sleeps 2
  • 1 queen bed
  • Breakfast supplied
  • 11am checkout
  • EV charger
  • Rainwater tank

Inside the Haus

One open room with a full kitchen, a queen bed and a pair of red leather recliners. The bathroom has a rain shower and a bathtub with forest and water views.

Breakfast is in the kitchen

A fully equipped kitchen with quality cookware, bespoke local pottery and lovely glassware. Make the coffee in the mokapot or the French press, or pick from the loose-leaf teas.

  • Fresh-ground coffee
  • Local bread
  • Milk and eggs
  • Tasmanian jam

Neighbours with feathers and spines

Your private deck is the place to watch the wildlife. Currawongs land on the rail and chicks wander the grass. In October, going by Heidi’s Instagram, the echidna came back.

Sounds of a running spring down the gully. Very warm and cosy inside with crochet blankets.Alice, Airbnb
