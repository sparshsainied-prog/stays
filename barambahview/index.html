Barambah View Cottage

A one bedroom cottage on the vineyard at Nuova Scuola Wines, Redgate, looking over the Barambah Valley.

“Stunning views, the Milky Way, delicious wine and great coffee.”
Ali, guest review on Airbnb, October 2026

The cottage

A self-contained cabin among the vines, a short walk from the cellar door. Breakfast is included, and you can order a charcuterie board to your room if you'd rather stay on the couch with a glass of wine than drive out for dinner.

The cellar door is next door

Stefano and Sarah make the wine here, working with old European varieties that suit the South Burnett climate: Verdelho, Viognier, Rosato, Barbera, Tempranillo. The cellar door opens by appointment, and you can walk to it from the cottage.

Private tasting, $5 a head

Taste the current wines with the winemakers. Redeemable on your first bottle.

Weekend wine experience, $30 a head

A vineyard tour with a glass of bubbles, a hosted tasting with Stefano, an Italian-style tagliere board and a taste of the award-winning fortified Verdelho.

How to book

  1. Pick your dates. Prices start from $150 a night for two.
  2. Booking for two but want the sofa bed made up? Book for three and add a note.
  3. Book at least 24 hours before you arrive.
  4. Want a tasting? Book it separately with the cellar door.
