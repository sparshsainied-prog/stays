The dairy

A dairy once stood here. It has been rebuilt on the same spot and fitted out from old things found and restored, so it keeps the feel of the original with everything you need for a slow stay.

You have it to yourselves. Lynne and Paul live in the main house, with its own entrance and parking.

The farm

Ten acres, with stock in the paddocks and Winnie the border collie at the door. Wander the vegetable patch for whatever is in season, and take eggs from the chickens when they are laying.

Rain water, solar power and a septic system. The Wi‑Fi is slow and comes and goes.
