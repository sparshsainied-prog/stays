Tied By Teila

Weddings, vow renewals and naming ceremonies across Mount Gambier and the Limestone Coast.

"I just simply love love"

Teila is a civil marriage celebrant living in Mount Gambier. As a little girl she was mesmerised by all things weddings: the dress, the rings, the celebration.

She always thought she'd become a celebrant when she retired. Then, at the lowest point of her life, a funeral celebrant helped her through her grief, and she decided she wouldn't wait to celebrate love.

"Your event isn't a business transaction to me." She'll stand by you, laugh with you, cry happy tears with you, and be your calm at the end of the aisle. She's a lover of coffee and wine, and your first meeting is over one or the other.

So you're ready to book your celebrant. What's next?

Teila's own steps, from first chat to I do.

  1. 1

    A first chat

    To make sure Teila is free on your date, and to set a time to meet.

  2. 2

    Coffee, or wine

    You meet, and fill in a booking form and your Notice of Intended Marriage.

  3. 3

    Designing your ceremony

    Teila sends a Couples Questionnaire, if you'd like one.

  4. 4

    Your first draft

    Yours to read at least two weeks before the wedding.

  5. 5

    Your final draft

    With any changes you asked for, at least a week before.

  6. 6

    A rehearsal or final meeting

    You sign the last legal form and walk through the ceremony together.

  7. 7

    The big day arrives and you get married

    Teila lodges the paperwork afterwards.
