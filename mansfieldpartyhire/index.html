Mansfield Party Hire and Events

After dark

Marquees, furniture, styling and on-the-day coordination for weddings in the High Country.

Kim and Nathan Peterson

Kim and Nathan have reinvented the business, with a focus on service, good gear and fair rates. It has been running for over twenty years.

Kim has extensive experience in events and also coordinates and plans weddings. If you need catering, flowers, photography or accommodation, she can link you with local suppliers and partners.

Based in Mansfield, serving Bonnie Doon, Eildon, Jamieson, Mt Buller, Benalla, Shepparton, Alexandra, Euroa, Yea, Kyneton, Wangaratta, Bright, Tatong and Swanpool.

What you can hire

Everything from the marquee to the salt and pepper mills.

Marquees

Clearspan and clear roof structure marquees, pagodas, peg and pole and frame marquees, with window walls and silk lining. Also the Hamptons marquee and clear roof panelling.

3 x 3 m up to 12 x 24 m and 10 x 45 m

  • Furnishings

    Chairs and covers, timber crossback chairs, tables, dance floors, flooring, picket or wrought iron fencing, and post and rope aisles.

  • Decor

    Linen, runners, table numbers and candleware. Kim can theme the whole event with you.

    Crockery, glassware and silverware, from champagne flutes to silver cake servers.
