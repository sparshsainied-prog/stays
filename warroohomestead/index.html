A farm homestead above the Murrumbidgee, for up to eight.

Four bedrooms, open fires, a salt water pool and a wide veranda looking down to the river.

"Sunset turns the Murrumbidgee River pink some nights."

Watch it from the veranda or the pool.

The homestead

Polished floorboards and original fireplaces, with velvet curtains from floor to ceiling in the dining room, lounge and main bedroom. The kitchen has new double Westinghouse ovens, a Miele dishwasher and an espresso machine.

Sleeps 8
Four bedrooms and five beds, including two queen rooms and a twin room with single beds. A cot for a baby on top.
Rooms
Formal lounge and dining rooms, a TV room, a sunroom and a country kitchen. A 65 inch Apple TV in the lounge.
Bathroom
Shower with a copper rain head, a clawfoot bath and a toilet, plus a second toilet.
Warm
Open fires, and reverse cycle central heating and cooling.
Also
Fast wifi, good 4G, a washing machine and parking.

Dinner by the fire

In winter, eat in front of the open fire. In summer, open the French doors onto the veranda and the pool.

The second bedroom looks up to the shearing shed, and you can watch the sun come up from the bed through its French windows.

Veranda and pool

The veranda has a hooded gas barbecue, a table for ten and chairs facing down to the river. Past it, the salt water pool is long enough to swim laps.

A working farm

Sheep and cattle graze the paddocks round the house, among them the farm's Belted Galloways, black at both ends with a white band round the middle.

"Like to feel calm? Come meet the Belties. Space to forget about everything."
