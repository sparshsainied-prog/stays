Inside the studio

  • Two bedrooms. Two guests wanting both rooms pay A$30 a stay extra.
  • A full kitchen with dishwasher, quality glassware and pantry basics.
  • Rain shower, washing machine and an undercover clothesline.
  • Wifi and a Smart TV for your own Netflix.
  • Undercover parking for one car, more if you need it.
  • Linen, seasonal doonas, shampoo, body wash and washing powder supplied.
