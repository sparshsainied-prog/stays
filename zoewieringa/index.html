Zoe Wieringa

Marriage celebrant for simple, relaxed ceremonies, with a professional but bubbly touch.

Like someone you already know

"The happiness and warmth that surrounds a wedding ceremony is like nothing else, it makes me slightly giddy."

Zoe is a Commonwealth registered marriage celebrant. She gets to know you both before the day, so the ceremony reflects your personalities, your relationship and the little things that make your story yours. Guests often assume you've been friends for years.

On the day she brings a calm, reassuring energy, so you can relax and soak in every moment.
