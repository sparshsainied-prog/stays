Old SaltySHACK

A beach shack among the gums above Binalong Bay, on Tasmania's east coast, first drawn up in 1993. Three bedrooms, a big deck, and the Bay of Fires down the hill.

Perfect for a morning cuppa on the deck.

From the shack's own Instagram

Walk the 1993 plan

This is the shack's original drawing, from the photos on their listing. Scroll, and walk through it room by room.

Original floor plan, 1993. Approved by the Municipality of Break O'Day in 1994.

Sleeps six

Three bedrooms and two bathrooms, with linen and towels supplied. Timber floors and pine ceilings throughout, and windows on the bay.

Swipe or scroll sideways for every room.

Bring the dog

The yard is fenced, so dogs can come too. Off the deck there's a firepit for marshmallows, and the beach is a short walk down the hill.

Fast internet (gigabit NBN) and a desk if work comes along. Parking on site and self check-in.

Down the hill, the Bay of Fires

White sand, clear water and granite boulders covered in orange lichen, a short walk or drive from the deck.
