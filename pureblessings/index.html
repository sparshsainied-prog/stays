Melissa Sloane, celebrant, Ballarat

Pure Blessings Celebrancy

Celebrating the circle of life, locally, nationally and internationally.

Pulling beers by the age of eight

Melissa was born into the hospitality industry and grew up in her family's pubs and hotels.

For a number of years she was the wedding coordinator in the family hotels. In that time she watched hundreds of priests and celebrants at work, and formed strong opinions about how she would write and present a ceremony, given the chance.

She had wanted to be a celebrant for over 20 years. COVID gave her the push, and she qualified in 2022.

  • 30+ yearsof public speaking, including as emcee at lots of weddings
  • 2022Cert IV in Celebrancy, The Gordon
  • JPJustice of the Peace (Qualified)
  • Two homesBallarat, and a place in Townsville where her family is

Every ceremony in the circle of life

It starts with you

Melissa believes a ceremony should never be "merely the necessary part of the day". It should be interactive, interesting, enjoyable and memorable, for you and your guests.

Weddings

Highly personalised and engaging ceremonies that reflect your personalities, values and vision.

Commitment ceremonies

Not a legal marriage, but a way to celebrate your commitment in front of the people you love.

Vow renewals

"Marry me again." For a milestone, or a stronger couple on the other side of a hard time.

Naming days

"Let's welcome your child to the world in style!" For a birth, a blended family or an adoption.

Pet ceremonies

Pet naming days, pet farewells and, yes, pet weddings.

Funerals and memorials

A personal tribute that reflects the life of the person you love, led with sensitivity and empathy.
