Judith White

Marriage celebrant for the Hunter Valley, Newcastle and the Central Coast.

A calm head, a warm heart and a cheeky sense of humour.

A proud Pommie in the Hunter Valley

Judith swapped England for Australia in 2008, and now happily calls the Hunter Valley home.

She describes herself as the reassuring calm of your mum or favourite auntie, with the quick wit of your bestie. She knows when to bring the laughs, when to bring the tissues, and how to keep everyone relaxed and included.

Your ceremony is all about you. Judith takes the time to get to know you both and your love story, then writes a ceremony that is personal and full of you.

Three ways to say I do

Every package includes a ceremony written for you. Ask Judith for current packages and prices.

Intimate and Special

Up to 6 guests, witnesses included

Minimal fuss, anywhere you choose. Judith writes your ceremony, prints your personal vows, does all the legal paperwork and runs an online rehearsal.

Sweet and Loving

Up to 20 guests

For backyard, casual, beach and surprise weddings. Special vows and ceremony written for you, a rehearsal at your venue where access allows, and a presentation copy of your ceremony and certificate.

Tailor-made for Your Love Story

20 guests or more

For venue weddings and bigger backyard days. Everything above, plus poems and readings and a ritual or tradition of your choice.

Always included

  • A first chat by phone or video, with no commitment
  • A meeting for your paperwork and Notice of Intended Marriage
  • Unlimited phone, email and video calls
  • Travel up to 100km, and Judith there at least 30 minutes early
