"How do I write my vows?"

"The same way you eat an elephant. Small bites."

  1. Months beforePut the first things that come into your head on paper.
  2. As the months go byAdd to those thoughts.
  3. Two to three weeks outPull it together into what you want to say.
  4. ThenPut it away, and come back to it a week later.
  5. On the dayThink of it as a word gift you give your partner. Keep it secret until then.

Questions couples ask Sue

How much say do we get?
A lot, and Sue encourages it. Some parts have to be there to meet the legal requirements. Around those, she'll guide you to a ceremony that's yours.
How long is the ceremony?
Most civil ceremonies run 10 to 15 minutes, plus 5 to 7 for signing. Allow about 30 minutes from start to finish.
When do we need to start the paperwork?
Your Notice of Intended Marriage has to be lodged with your celebrant at least one calendar month before the wedding, and it's valid for 18 months. Sue prepares it with you.

Is your date free?

Tell Sue your date and venue and she'll get back to you. You can also call her on 0410 933 235.
