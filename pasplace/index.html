Pa's Place

The house was built in the late 1800s and shaped by generations of farmers. Rick Mahnken's family has farmed North Lilydale since the late 1870s. He and Liz now run the farm, and they've furnished Pa's Place with vintage and upcycled pieces.

Summerlea Farm

A hundred acres at North Lilydale with a small herd of grass-fed Angus cattle.

In 2020 the family started planting thousands of native trees along the Second River with NRM North, to protect the Giant Freshwater Crayfish, which is native to northern Tasmania. Fees from farm tours go back into that work.

Private walking tracks run through the regenerating bush and along the river banks. Ask Liz to arrange a walk.
