Berry House

A heritage-listed estate built in 1857, on five acres. The Cottage, in its grounds, sleeps two to four.

Across the dam, the old sandstone stables.

Sheep graze right up to the sandstone walls.

The Cottage

A self-contained cottage on the grounds of the estate, for two to four people. Berry House was built in 1857 by John Eales: solid sandstone walls, 14-foot ceilings and old timber, now heritage listed.

Use it as a base for Morpeth and the rest of the Hunter, or don't leave the five acres at all.

Five acres

Old established gardens, a dam, a veggie patch, the stables and paddocks. Lounge by the pool, sit in the sauna, then take a drink out to the fire pit as the sun goes down.

Who else lives here

Sheep, chooks, dogs and frogs. Say hello to the sheep, and collect a few farm-fresh eggs for breakfast.

On the first day of spring this year they posted nine ducklings out on the Berry House dam.
