Let's relax, have fun and get you married

"There is no need for structured scripts that allow no room for your individuality."

Jacklyn, or Jackie, meets you over a coffee and helps you plan everything, from your vows and readings to the paperwork. She marries couples, holds commitment ceremonies and renews vows.
