Daybreak Dugout

One of the oldest dugouts in White Cliffs, renovated, with the bedrooms underground.

“We enjoyed the experience of sleeping in a cave like environment.”
Helen, guest review on Airbnb

Two levels

You come in through the stone cottage on top of the hill. Walk on through and you're underground.

Above ground

The stone cottage

A new kitchen with everything you need to cook, and a big window over the view from the hill. The bathroom is up here too, with a rain shower for the outback dust. Air conditioning in the kitchen, a barbecue and fire pit outside.

Underground

The dugout

The main bedroom and a living area sit in the rock, with rooms shaped by the stone walls and hallways. Further in, the second bedroom has two single beds under a high ceiling. The couch folds out into a sofa bed.

Good to know

  • The bedrooms have no doors. The stone walls give them some privacy, and the main bedroom can be seen from the living area.
  • Coming with children? Say so when you book. The dugout is set up a little differently for families. Bring your own port-a-cot.
  • The driveway is uphill and a bit rocky. A 2WD is fine; take it slowly.
  • The nearest big shops are a 600 km round trip, so bring what you need.
  • No pets and no smoking inside.

How to book

  1. Pick your dates. The nightly price is for two people.

  3. Tell the host if children are coming.
