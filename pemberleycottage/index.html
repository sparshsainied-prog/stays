Malmsbury, in the Macedon Ranges

A farm cottage above the Malmsbury Reservoir.

Sleeps six, with a king, a queen and two singles. The reservoir, the bluestone viaduct and Mount Macedon are all in view from the deck.

The whole cottage is yours.

Up a tree-lined driveway on a working grazing farm, with the cows, sheep and chooks next door.

One evening in the outdoor bath.

There's a claw-foot bath out on the deck, looking over the water. Run it before sunset and stay in as long as you like.

Sunset

Fill the bath while the sun goes down behind the reservoir.

Last light

The sky over the water goes pink and orange.

"We loved feeding the cows oranges and having a bonfire as the sunset!" Jen, Airbnb

Moonrise

Stay in for the stars, and watch the moon come up over the reservoir.

Morning

Run it again for sunrise, with the mist still on the paddocks.

"Beautiful waking up to frost and a gorgeous sunrise!" Emily, Airbnb

Inside the cottage

Big windows face the water, so you see it from the lounge, the kitchen and the dining table. The rooms have old timber and iron beds, and there's a wood fire for cold nights.

"The views are absolutely spectacular, no matter the weather."

Naomi, Airbnb, November 2026

The farm

Pemberley is a grazing and cropping property run by third-generation farmers. The Highland cattle, sheep and chooks are pets, and guests can feed them if they ask.

  • "The highlight was waiting for the cows to come close enough the feed them oranges and an extra special treat was feeding baby Mog some milk."

    Gemma, Airbnb, September 2026

  • "Olivia was also kind enough to let our kids collect the eggs and pick some flowers too."

    Ilka and Martin, Airbnb, April 2026
