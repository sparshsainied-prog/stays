Abels Bay, Huon Valley, Tasmania

A shack on the water, with the fire going.

Two bedrooms, an indoor fire and a backyard that runs down to a private bay. Cygnet is 15 minutes away, Hobart about an hour.

Inside the shack

Made for two, with room for four. The lounge sits round the wood fire with the bay through the windows, and the main bedroom has the same view. Breakfast for the first morning is there when you arrive: bread, butter, free range eggs and milk.

"We felt welcomed and relaxed as soon as we arrived with beautiful fresh bread and eggs, wood ready to light a fire and a cosy, arty, coastal, vibe."

Katrine, on Airbnb, September 2026

Out the back

In the shack's own words, "Outside is really the showstopper." The yard runs down through the gorse to the water, with a fire pit, a table at the shore and access to a bay only residents use.

A fire pit above the bay

Light the fire pit and sit out under the gums above the water. Walking tracks, cellar doors and cideries are all a short drive away in the Huon Valley.

One guest in September met an echidna in the garden. Wildlife is about at night, so drive slowly from dusk to dawn.
