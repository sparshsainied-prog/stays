Three packages

Studio Delilah has three packages, each quoted individually based on the style and flowers you'd like.

  • Bridal Party Blooming: bridal bouquet, bridesmaid flowers, groom and groomsmen's buttonholes, delivery to up to two locations. Optional extras: flower girl petals, ring bearer buttonhole and petal confetti.
  • Intimate Day: bridal party flowers, ceremony flowers, delivery to up to three locations, ceremony setup.
  • Out with a Bang!: bridal party flowers, ceremony floral styling, reception floral styling, delivery to up to four locations, ceremony and reception setup.

Midnight or following-day pack-down is available at an additional cost.

Before you ask

Every wedding is quoted on its own, from the style and the flowers you'd like. Cassidy is upfront about cost from the start.

Is there a minimum spend?
No. A single bouquet for an elopement or full styling for a big celebration, every enquiry is welcome.
Can we work to a budget?
Yes. Cassidy puts your budget into the arrangements that will make the biggest difference.
Can we ask for certain flowers?
Yes. What's available changes with the season and the market, so she'll find your favourites or suggest something close.
Can ceremony flowers move to the reception?
Where it's practical. Your welcome sign can become the seating chart, and arrangements can move to the reception.
Fresh, dried or faux?
Fresh, seasonal flowers first, with dried or preserved pieces where they suit.
Will you come outside Perth?
Yes. Extra travel and delivery fees may apply.
How early should we book?
Six to twelve months ahead, especially in peak season. Ask about late dates anyway.
Can we meet first?
Yes. Talk through your flowers, colours and the look you want.
