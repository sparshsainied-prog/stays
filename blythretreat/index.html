An outdoor bath in the gums.

A two bedroom house in the middle of Bruny Island, for up to four. The bath is outside and private, with the bush all round and water views.

Run it deep and look up.

Towering gums all round, and Bruny Island Cheese Co is a five minute walk away.

The house

New, sunny and open plan, styled for comfort: linen, throw rugs and cushions in both bedrooms, and a big lounge to sink into with your music on the soundbar.

Sleeps 4
One bedroom with a queen bed, one with a double.
Kitchen
Modern and open plan, with good dinnerware. A few pantry staples are there when you arrive.
Bathroom
Contemporary, with Appelles body wash, shampoo and conditioner, towels and face washers.
Also
Laundry, free wifi, outdoor furniture. Non smoking.

Outside

More than one place to sit. The fire pit looks out to the water, the BBQ is on the main deck, and the bath is private, with towering gums around it.

  • Fire pit with water views
  • BBQ on the main deck
  • Private outdoor bath

Who's in the gums

Blyth Retreat is known for its birdlife. From the bath or the deck, look out for:

  1. Kookaburras
  2. Native black cockatoos
  3. Wrens
  4. Swift parrots
