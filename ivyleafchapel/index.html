The Bungalow The garden Check dates

141 Miles Street, Tenterfield

A 1915 bungalow, and a chapel grown from Boston ivy.

The Bungalow sleeps up to four, with breakfast in the room and a wood fire in the lounge. Kim's garden covers 1.5 acres, five minutes' walk from the main street.

In autumn the ivy turns red.

Kim designed the chapel with her eldest son after a trip to Japan. It went up in the winter of 2016, and the ivy has covered the frame since.

In winter you can see the frame.

Frost on the hedges outside. Inside, the fire is lit and the bed has an electric blanket.

The Bungalow

The front door opens into your own lounge and its fireplace. A second door leads to the bedroom, with its ensuite and walk-in robe. The verandah looks over the garden.

Kim's garden

Kim bought the house in 2010, when the front yard was a stretch of lawn and one old pine. She is a landscape gardener, and she planted the rest.

Out the front is a formal parterre of low box hedges, lavender and berberis. The back garden is softer, with grasses and salvia under deciduous trees and a few spiral conifers. The chapel stands in what used to be the back paddock.

Better Homes and Gardens filmed here with Graham Ross in December 2022.

Weddings and long lunches

Couples have married in the chapel, and Kim hosts small functions, high teas and long table events in the garden. Ask her about dates.
