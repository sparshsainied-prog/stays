The house

The Blue Hut sits on Sellicks Beach Road with a nature reserve round it and no direct neighbours. There's a deck front and back, ocean views, and room to park the car, the boat or the jet skis inside the gates.

Sleeps 6
Three bedrooms and two bathrooms. The main bedroom has a walk-in robe and an ensuite.
Cook
A full kitchen with a dishwasher, and a dining table that seats eight.
Warm or cool
Ducted reverse cycle air conditioning, and ceiling fans in the bedrooms and living areas.
Linen
Beds made up, towels, bath mats and robes. Electric blankets in winter.
Outside
A deck with a BBQ and outdoor dining, a back lawn and a nest swing.
Arrive
Self check-in with a smart lock. A welcome pack of locally sourced products is waiting.
Charge
A 3.5kW EV charger, on request.

Getting here

Sellicks Beach is on the Fleurieu, under the hills where the cliffs meet the gulf. Willunga's Saturday farmers' market and the McLaren Vale cellar doors are a short drive.
