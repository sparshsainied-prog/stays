The cottage Getting here Check dates

An 1860s cottage among the vines at Carey Gully.

Two king bedrooms, a wood fire and a gin nook, 25 minutes from Adelaide.

Breakfast on the front verandah, looking at the vines.

Out the back, sheep graze in the next paddock. At dusk, kangaroos come through.

Inside the cottage

Built in the 1860s and restored, with Jarrah floors, a record player and room for four. Firewood is supplied.

  • LoungeWood fire with firewood, kindling and firelighters supplied. Smart TV, record player.
  • Dining roomA round table for four and the gin nook, with a welcome bottle of Dapper Chap gin.
  • Two king bedroomsOne looks out to the vineyard. The other is light filled, on Jarrah floors.
  • KitchenFully equipped, with a DeLonghi pod machine and an AeroPress.
  • BathroomSeparate toilet, al.ive body products, a Dyson Supersonic hairdryer.
  • And the restLaundry with washer and dryer, reverse-cycle air conditioning, lockable bike storage.

On the bench when you arrive

  • Uraidla Bakery sourdough and butter
  • Farm-fresh eggs
  • Beerenberg jam and honey
  • A bottle of CRFT wine. Their cellar door is 250m up the road.
  • 200ml of Dapper Chap gin for the gin nook
  • Urban Brew compostable coffee pods, made in South Australia

A quarter acre of garden

Lawns, a dining terrace that looks over the vineyard, an undercover patio with a four-burner Beefmaster barbecue, and a fire pit ringed with Adirondack chairs. Bocce and Connect 4 are on the lawn.

Kangaroos come through at dusk, kookaburras call from the gums, and now and then there's a koala.
