Ceremonies with Tanya

Marriage celebrant, Jervis Bay NSW

The show must go on

Weddings on the beach, on a boat, on a headland or in a garden, from big days to small elopements.

"I grew up on stage, dancing, acting and singing (although some would say, poorly)."
Tanya

After all those performances, Tanya says she is still as nervous as you are on the big day. She became a celebrant after watching a close friend of the couple marry them, and loved how personal it felt.

She wants to hear how you met, when you got engaged and how you picture the day. Then you can use her words, write your own, or mix the two. She has plenty of sample ceremonies and poems to start from.

Where would you like it?

  • A beach
  • A boat
  • A headland
  • A floral garden
  • Your favourite park

And when: sunrise, sunset, or anywhere in between. In Tanya's words, "the strike of midnight if you fancy." She travels the beaches of the South Coast and up to the Mid North Coast.

Your ceremony

About 20 to 30 minutes, longer or shorter if you prefer

  1. The Monitummust be included
  2. Your vowsmust be included, no need to memorise
  3. Readings or poemsup to two
  4. The giving awayoptional
  5. The asking"I do" or "I will"
  6. The ring exchangetwo rings, one or none
  7. Declaration and kissyou're married

Add a ritual if you like: sand ceremony, ring warming, hand fasting, butterfly or dove release.

What Tanya brings

  • All the legal paperwork, then registration with Births Deaths and Marriages
  • A personalised ceremony, designed with you
  • A PA system so every guest hears every word, and for your music
  • A portable signing table and chair with a white cloth
  • A commemorative marriage certificate
  • Two witnesses, if you are eloping

Renewal of vows

Rewrite your vows and make new promises. Any little ones who came along since the first "I do" can be part of it too.

Commitment ceremonies

Vows, rings, readings and music, without gender or religious bias.

Naming days

Welcome a child, name guardians or mentors, and take home a naming certificate.

Book a chat with Tanya

The first chat is obligation free. If you book:

  1. You lodge your Notice of Intended Marriage with Tanya at least one month before the wedding, and up to 18 months ahead.
  2. A booking fee holds your date.
  3. The balance is due 7 days before the wedding.
