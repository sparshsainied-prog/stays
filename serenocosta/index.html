Red Head, Hallidays Point, on the Barrington Coast

A four-bedroom beach house in the rainforest above Red Head beach.

Sleeps 12. It's the last house on a quiet cul de sac, with bush on two sides and the beach down the steps.

"A tree-house by the sea."

A guest, quoted on the Barrington Coast listing

Walk the beach to Blackhead.

Out the door

Head out the front and into the rainforest, where tracks lead down to the beaches. Guests have met a wallaby on the way.

"The children being able to get close to the local kookaburras." Wendy, Airbnb

Down the steps

A Parks staircase of 40-odd steps takes you onto the sand at Red Head. Go early, while the sun is still low on the water.

"There is a lookout very close by where we were able to watch the sunrise each morning." Wendy, Airbnb

Blackhead

About ten minutes south along the sand is Blackhead, where the surf club patrols in season. Check the flags before you swim, then walk back, or drive it in five.

"A safe, patrolled surf beach." Yvonne, Airbnb

Any weather

The walk is good in any weather. Here's Blackhead with a storm rolling in.

"No matter what the weather the beach is magnificent." Nicole, Sereno Costa

Room for two families

The living is upstairs, looking out over the coast. Downstairs has its own lounge, so the kids or a second family can spread out.

Upstairs

  • Open plan kitchen, dining and lounge, with an induction cooktop, oven and dishwasher
  • The king room, with a large ensuite and its own balcony
  • A wrap-around balcony with two big outdoor tables and a gas barbecue with a rotisserie

Downstairs

  • Two queen rooms and a bunk room with two sets of bunks
  • A second lounge with a sofa bed, board games and books
  • A bathroom with a big bath, underfloor heating for winter, and the laundry
  • An outdoor hot and cold shower for sandy feet
