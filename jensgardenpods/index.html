Inside a pod

Each pod is fully self-contained, with a living and sleeping space, a kitchenette and a full bathroom. They sleep two in a queen bed. Coming with friends? Book both.

  • Kitchenette. Kettle, toaster, microwave, bar fridge and a Nespresso machine with pods.
  • Bathroom. Large shower with instant gas hot water, so long showers are fine.
  • Comfort. Reverse cycle air conditioning, a smart TV and free wifi.
  • By the bed. A charging station for all your devices.
  • Parking. A dedicated space for each pod.
  • Checkout. No cleaning fee. Wash up, lock the door, and we do the rest.

Breakfast in, fire pit out

Continental breakfast is included: cereals, fresh bread on arrival, butter, spreads, tea and coffee. For a small fee we'll leave a Cook-Your-Own pack of bacon, tomato, mushrooms and fresh farm eggs for the BBQ.

Out the front, your private patio has its own table and fire pit. The BBQ and kitchen under the central pergola are for guests only.
