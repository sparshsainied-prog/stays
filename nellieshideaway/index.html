The house

Two levels and two living areas, so a group can spread out. Upstairs, the kitchen, dining and living room open on to the balcony, next to the main bedroom and its ensuite. Downstairs are three more bedrooms, a family room, the bathroom and the laundry.

Getting here

Normanville is on the western Fleurieu, about an hour and ten minutes' drive from Adelaide. The beach and the jetty are a few minutes away, and the Kangaroo Island ferry leaves from Cape Jervis.
