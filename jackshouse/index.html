Inside

Hardwood floors, reverse-cycle air conditioning, king beds and good linen. The long open living and dining room looks out to the garden.

Sleeps
Six. The rate covers two guests; extra guests are charged per night.
Beds
Two kings and two singles. The singles join into a third king if you ask ahead.
Breakfast
Continental provisions for each morning, plus tea, coffee pods, milk and sugar.
Bathroom
One, with linen and robes supplied.
Staying in
Unlimited WiFi, four TVs, DVDs and games, and a washing machine.
Parking
Free, on the property.

The backyard

A big, fully fenced garden with a fire pit, a BBQ and a covered outdoor table. Guests keep mentioning how much room the dogs had.

Clare on foot

Leave the car in the drive. The house is in a quiet street in the middle of town, and Main Street, two wineries and the Riesling Trail are all a short walk or ride away.

  1. The front doorJack's House, Clare
  2. Main StreetFive minutes' walk to the shops, cafes, restaurants and supermarkets.
  3. Two wineriesAn easy walk, or hop in the car for a day of cellar doors.
  4. The Riesling TrailA few minutes by bike or about 15 minutes on foot. Ride on as far as Auburn, past cellar doors, bakeries and restaurants.
  5. Clare Valley Lifestyle CentreAn easy stroll past a park and playground, for the pool, gym and squash courts.
  6. Clare Golf ClubTen minutes in the car.
