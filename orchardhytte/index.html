Orchard Hytte

Say it hee-ta.

A 1950s workers' cabin on a fourth-generation apple farm at Pozieres, in the Granite Belt. Wood heater inside, a private hot tub outside, and your dog is welcome.

Check datesWalk the farm

Hytte is Norwegian for cabin. This one was built for the farm's workers.

It sits 50 metres from the original farmhouse, inside a 100-year-old working apple orchard that is now in its fourth generation of the same family.

The cabin is small and cosy: one bedroom for two, a kitchen, a wood heater and a fully fenced yard. Walking tracks wind through the farm.

Start at the cabin

Two farm dogs often meet guests at the door. Guest reviews name them Woody and Snowy.

Inside, it's all timber and lamplight

Ply walls, a pine ceiling, timber floors and a wood heater in the corner. Guests say it warms the room fast. The bathroom has a glass shower and Aesop at the basin.

  • 2 guests, 1 bedroom, 1 bed, 1 bathroom
  • Wood heater, plus split-system air conditioning
  • Kitchen and Wi-Fi
  • Private hot tub, open all year
  • Fire pit and outdoor dining
  • Fully fenced backyard and free parking

Bring the dog

Pets are allowed, and the backyard is fully fenced, so your dog can wander while you sit out. The farm walking tracks are yours to use, and the two resident collies usually come along.
