Serenity by the Lake

A boathouse for two on the foreshore of Lake Macquarie, at the very tip of the Marks Point peninsula.

Leave the world at the top of the stairs.

Four flights down from Marks Point Road there's a self-contained studio, a lawn, a lakeside fire pit and your own jetty. Kayaks, paddleboards and fishing gear are all provided.

We designed the bedroom to be the ultimate relaxation zone.

The L-shaped lounge faces the oversized window, and the bed looks straight out at the lake. Keep scrolling and step through it.

Morning glow

Wake up to the water without lifting your head from the pillow. The double blinds stay down if you'd rather sleep in.

Sunset off the jetty

The Chandon is already in the fridge. Take it down to the end of the jetty and watch the light change on the lake.

The fire pit after dark

Light the lakeside fire pit, toast marshmallows and listen to the water.

Already in the fridge when you arrive
