Murrumbateman, Yass Valley, NSW

A cottage on a hobby farm, 30 minutes from Canberra.

Two bedrooms, two bathrooms and a front deck over the paddock where the sheep and alpacas graze.

Press the button on the post.

The front gate opens for you and closes itself 30 seconds later. The cottage sits on its own fenced acre at the end of the drive.

Inside the cottage

Self-contained and all yours: an open kitchen and dining room, a lounge with a smart TV, two large queen bedrooms and two bathrooms, one of them an ensuite off the main bedroom.

A welcome breakfast basket is waiting when you arrive.

Getting here

Murrumbateman is in the Yass Valley wine country, north-west of Canberra. The cottage is a good base for weddings at Shaw Estate: guests who dine at Shaw Restaurant get a voucher for a bottle of Shaw Estate wine to take home.
