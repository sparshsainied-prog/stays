Dawesley Cottage

The old copper smelter office at Dawesley, now a stone cottage for two on Maddie and Milos's farm.

Check dates See inside

Get away from the city to a quiet stone cottage.

Maddie and Milos renovated the cottage as a retreat for couples. One big room under the old roof trusses, a four-poster bed facing the fireplace, a deep bath, and the paddocks outside the door.

It's a 20 minute drive to Hahndorf and Woodside for long lunches and the cellar doors.

The copper smelter's office.

  1. 1847A copper smelter starts on a little stream that became Scott's Creek, now Dawesley Creek.
  2. 1857William Bower Dawes lays out the township. The cottage, one of its first buildings, was the smelter's office.
  3. After the smeltersMr and Mrs Hawthorn turn the more than 300 acres into an award-winning merino stud.
  4. 2019Maddie and Milos move in on Australia Day weekend. Since then they've renovated the old office for two.
  5. TonightFirewood is supplied, so you can light the fire whenever you like.

One room, under the old trusses.

Sleeps two. A four-poster bed hung with sheer curtains, the stone and brick fireplace, a couch, a small kitchen with a Nespresso machine, and a renovated bathroom with a freestanding bath.

Breakfast is on the farm.

Breakfast provisions are left for your first morning, and the chooks on the farm lay the eggs. You can feed them and collect a few yourself.

"Milos ensured that there was breakfast provisions and a lovely loaf of baked bread for our enjoyment."

Montana, Airbnb

Sheep that like a cuddle.

The farm has sheep, chooks and horses, plus the wild ones that come by. Walk the paddock hills to a lookout for a picnic, and stay out for the sunset.
