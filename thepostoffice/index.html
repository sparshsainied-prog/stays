Inside

Restored to suit its era and filled with antiques, oil paintings and unusual finds. Dark panelled walls, 4.2 metre pressed tin ceilings and a wood pellet fire in the middle of the living room.

  • Kept from 1913Pressed tin ceilings, arched sash windows, brass fixtures, the cedar counter and the walk-in bank safe.
  • Two bedroomsThe main bed sits behind a velvet curtain and a half wall. The second has a bed nook beside a cast iron bath.
  • BedsFlax linen, toppers and electric blankets.
  • BathroomUnderfloor heating and a large level-access shower.
  • KitchenA five-burner ILVE, a big SMEG fridge and freezer, and the old counter as the island bench.
  • SleepsMade for a couple, with room for up to four guests.

The first morning

Breakfast provisions for your first morning are waiting in the kitchen: milk, eggs, butter, jam, and a bread maker with the ingredients for a loaf.

The pantry has freshly ground coffee, organic herbal and black teas, hot chocolate, olive oil, salt, pepper and herbs. Stock up on the rest before you arrive, as Waratah's shops are limited.

Waratah, from the front step

A quiet town on the edge of the Tarkine, with a waterfall in the middle of it. It's not uncommon to see wallabies and wombats grazing nearby.
