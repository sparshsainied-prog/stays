"I am very much a people person"

Cathryn grew up in the Southern Highlands of NSW, and the South West of WA has been home for over 25 years.

She starts by getting to know you both, over a coffee or a glass of wine, then writes a ceremony that tells your story. Weddings and family are her two great loves, and she has officiated her own daughter's wedding.
