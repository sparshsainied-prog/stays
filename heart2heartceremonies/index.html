Quick answers

How far ahead should we book?
Most couples book 12 to 18 months ahead, especially for spring and Saturdays. If your date is sooner, still ask.
What is a Notice of Intended Marriage?
The legal form you lodge at least one month before your wedding. Marlee guides you through it.
How long is a ceremony?
Most run 20 to 30 minutes, depending on readings, rituals and personal touches.
Do we need to meet first?
Not at all. You can book straight away, or have an obligation free chat in person or on Zoom.
