Meet Kylee

A people person through and through, with a background in communications, events and talent management.

Kylee has spent years helping people present at their best and feel comfortable in the big moments. Her ceremonies are a blend of heart, laughter and careful preparation. The approach is warm and relaxed, and she is very detail focused, so nothing is left to chance.

She takes the time to get to know you and what matters to you most, then writes a ceremony around your values and your love story.

"I am THAT person who says 'If you love your job, you'll never have to work a day in your life'."

How it goes

  1. Get in touch

    It all starts here. Enquire and Kylee will get straight back to you.

  2. We'll meet

    An obligation free consult to talk about your dream wedding and the legals.

  3. We'll create

    Lots of inspiration and ideas, then together you create your own ceremony.

  4. Your wedding day

    Your ceremony, all you wished for and more. A rehearsal comes first, in the days before.

Every wedding ceremony includes

  • A first meeting, obligation free, to hear your ideas
  • All the legal paperwork, from the Notice of Intended Marriage to lodgement
  • Unlimited consultations with Kylee by email, phone or follow up meetings
  • Ideas, support and resources for your ceremony
  • Your ceremony written with you, from first draft to the final version you approve
  • A rehearsal at the venue in the days before
  • Her outfit planned with you, to suit your dress code
  • Arrival at least 40 minutes before you start
  • A professional PA system that plays your music from your phone or a USB
  • A signing table, tablecloth and two chairs if you need them
  • A printed presentation marriage certificate
  • A keepsake copy of your ceremony script
