The Suite

A French country wing of Michelle and Ron's home, set apart with its own entrance, made for two.

Wake to the valley from the king bed. On cool nights there's the gas log fire, and in the warmer months the pool looks over the hills. A young child can come too, with a kids mattress and sleeping bag ready.

A working truffle farm

Scotch Hill grows French black truffles under its oaks, and the dogs find them.

Fresh truffles come up from June to September. In winter, guests can join a truffle hunt with the dogs, then warm up at a fireside tasting. The rest of the year there are sheep at the fence and horses in the morning mist.
