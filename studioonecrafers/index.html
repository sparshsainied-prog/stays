Room for four

A self-contained apartment, lovingly updated, with its own secure entrance and parking for two cars out the front.

Sleeps 4
A king in bedroom 1, two king singles in bedroom 2. Cot and highchair on request.
Kitchen
Gas range cooker, electric oven, dishwasher, full-size fridge, microwave, pod coffee machine.
Bathroom
Two-person spa, separate shower, heated towel rails, robes.
Laundry
Washer and dryer behind full-length cupboard doors, and a clothesline in the courtyard.
Comfort
Reverse-cycle air conditioning, ceiling fans in both bedrooms, wifi, Netflix.
Times
Check in from 2pm, check out by 11am.

When you arrive

A taste of the Hills is waiting on the bench: locally made wine, cheese and biscuits. Breakfast for your first morning is in the kitchen too, with cereal, croissants, and local jam and juice.
