Wedding stages, floral arches and mirror pathways in Adelaide, styled by husband and wife Joshma and Praveen.

Your vision is our passion.

Josh Events is a family business, owned and run by Joshma and Praveen. They take the time to understand your vision and honour your traditions, then build a setting that feels like yours.

Their collection has premium artificial and real-touch flowers, backdrops and centrepieces, with dance floors and mirror pathways to go with them.

Based in Andrews Farm, in Adelaide's north. Weddings across Adelaide.
