Fitzroy Falls, Southern Highlands

One private lodge on a 70‑acre cattle farm.

Two bedrooms, four guests at most, and a farm tour with every stay. About an hour and a half south of Sydney.

Up the tree-lined drive.

Black post and rail fences either side. The lodge sits 300 metres from the road.

The only guest lodge on the farm.

Out of sight of the main house, with a big deck and an open wood fire.

Every stay comes with a farm tour.

Usually at 8am or 4pm, on whichever day suits you. It takes over an hour, in this order:

  1. Feed the poultry
  2. Feed the goats
  3. Feed the pigs
  4. Walk and groom the horses
  5. Out in the six-seat buggy to the cows

After that, the animals are all close to the lodge, so you can go and see them any time you like.

The lodge

Snowy mountains lodge on the outside, new and modern inside. It's heated, air conditioned and fully insulated, and professionally cleaned after every stay.

Sleeps 4
A strict limit of four guests.
Main room
A king bed and its own ensuite.
Bunk room
Two single bunks.
Kitchen
Gas oven and stove, microwave, coffee machine, and a separate living and dining area.
Deck
A big entertaining deck with an open wood fire, lounge seating, outdoor dining and a breakfast bar.
Unplugged
No wifi or Netflix. Two TVs and a DVD player. Telstra works fine.

The grounds

Three acres of gardens, and all of it is yours to use: the tennis court (racquets and balls are there), a basketball hoop, a trampoline, the gazebo and the barbecue. A small creek runs through the property.
