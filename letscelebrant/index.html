Marriage celebrant. She gets to know you both, then writes a ceremony that suits you.

A wife, a mumma and a celebrant

"I like to be able to get to know the couple so I can tailor a ceremony that is suited to them as individuals."

Ellise writes a ceremony script for each couple. She'll put in your funny and romantic moments and the moment you knew you'd met the one, and she helps with your vows, readings and the shape of the ceremony.

A religious tradition, a poem that matters or a song you love can all go in. So can a floral lei at a beach wedding, or a shout out to someone in the family.
