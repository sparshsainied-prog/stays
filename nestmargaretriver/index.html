Nest~Margaret River

A rebuilt shack in the bush for up to four, with a view to the olive grove, chickens about the place and ceramics from Jolene's studio on the shelves.

Through the french doors

A new kitchen, a 17th century farmhouse table, a wood fire for cool nights and a shelf of books in case you don't leave at all.

Built from the old shack

When Jolene and Paul bought the place, the cottage was rough but still charming. Jolene, a building designer by trade, drew a small, cosy house to replace it, and Paul rebuilt it, reusing as many of the original materials as they could.

That was 2012. In 2025 they renovated it again, with local trades and repurposed materials, and filled it with organic linen, rustic homewares, art and furniture. Jolene says it isn't pristine hotel-style: she prefers antique and interesting finishes. In the cooler months you hear rain on the tin roof; in summer the french doors stay open to the deck.

Two bedrooms, one bathroom, sleeps four

Good for a couple, a small family or two friends. There's a cot, highchair and baby bath if you ask.

Swipe or scroll sideways for every room.

Made in the studio up the hill

Jolene is a ceramicist. The cups, bowls and plates you use at Nest come from her studio, a short walk uphill. She also runs workshops and retreats.

A workshop while you stay

Staying two nights or more? Jolene can run a workshop for up to four of you, at the Nest kitchen table or up in her studio. Make a mug, a small vase or a little sculpture, and she fires it and posts it to you anywhere in Australia. From $650 for the group, materials included. Ask her first to check she's free.

The grounds are yours, up to the firebreak

Walk through the olive grove and the vegetable gardens, and say hello to the chickens if they're home. Guests keep mentioning the geese. Kangaroos come through often, there are plenty of birds, and the possums come out after dark.

There's a picnic basket and rug to use on the lawn, or to take down to Ellensbrook or Surfers Point.

Where it is

Nest sits right on Caves Road at Burnside, in the middle of the cape, with the 4WD trails of Kilcarnup close by. Beaches, surf breaks, wineries and Margaret River town are all a short drive.

Jolene has written up her favourite places to eat, swim and visit, and there's a book of her suggestions in the house.

Good to know

  • Rainwater only. Nest runs on tank water, so go easy in the shower.
  • Steps and a steep gravel drive. Park at the bottom of the hill if the top doesn't suit you.
  • On Caves Road. In busy times you may hear daytime traffic.
  • Laundry. Linen is washed on site, so the laundry usually isn't open to guests.
  • Heating and cooling. Wood fire, reverse cycle air con and ceiling fans in the living room, fans in each bedroom.
