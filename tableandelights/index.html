Table and Delights

Wedding planning, styling and on-the-day coordination with Dominika, across Shepparton and Central Victoria.

Dom

Dominika grew up in Poland and moved to Australia in December 2014 to be with her husband.

She worked in commercial law in the banking sector, then went after her creative and artistic side and started Table and Delights. She's also a mum of three. Couples call her Dom.

Her listing covers Shepparton and Central Victoria. One mother had her organise the venue decorations for her daughter's wedding in Canberra.

What Dom looks after

In her couples' words. Some hand her the whole day, some bring her in for the styling.

Styling

Tables, drapes, florals and signs, from soft white and sand to bright oranges and reds. She styles bridal showers as well as weddings.

"I was lucky enough to have Dom style both my bridal shower and our wedding." Vanessa D.

  • Planning

    From the first ideas through to the day, with the florist and the rest of the team organised along the way.

    "From organising our incredible florist to coordinating the entire ceremony and reception." Patricia R.

  • Suppliers and the venue

    She sources flowers and decorations, deals with suppliers and talks to the venue staff.

    "From sourcing flowers and decorations to managing difficult suppliers, she handled everything seamlessly." Abby H.

  • On the day

    She's there through the day, so you can relax and enjoy it.

    "On the day of the wedding, Dom was there for absolutely everything we needed." Abby H.

  • Small days too

    She sets up elopements as well as big receptions.

    "They pulled off our elopement setup even better than I had imagined." Tayla B.
