The Curated Life

Wedding planning, styling and flowers by Belinda Brett. Sydney, and wherever your day is.

Pared back luxury, inspired by fashion, interiors and design

Belinda launched The Curated Life after almost a decade in London, running PR and events for designer and luxury brands. She brings over 16 years of that work to every wedding.

The aim is a wedding that feels like walking into a stylish party. Belinda and her team plan it, style it and build the look around flowers from their in-house florist team.

Three ways to work with Belinda

There is no one size fits all package or price. Each design is tailored to your budget.
