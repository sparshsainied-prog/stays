Glenlynn, near Bridgetown, WA

A private cabin on the edge of the forest.

State forest on one side, rolling paddocks on the other, and no neighbours. Bridgetown is six minutes away.

"Nightfall is magical at the Cabin."

From the Forest Edge Cabin Instagram. At dusk the kangaroos and emus come out to feed.

The cabin

One generous studio-style room for two. The queen bed has picture windows on two sides, so the view keeps changing across the paddocks, and there's a window seat to read in.

"A gorgeous reading nook surrounded by windows and lovely outdoor bath."

Sarah, on Airbnb, November 2026

Sleeps 2
One queen bed in the main cabin. Room for up to 6 if you ask for the bunk room.
Warm
A wood heater inside for the cool evenings.
Bathroom
Private, across the covered verandah, about 5 metres from the cabin. Under-floor heating, a large shower and views. The laundry is out there too.
Outside
A deck for sundowners, and a bath under the sky.
Arrive
Self check-in from 3pm. Check out by 10am.

Bringing more people?

There's a separate bunk room with a double and a single, and a trundle that rolls out from under the queen. Ask for them when you book; they stay locked otherwise.

The nightly rate covers two. Each extra guest is A$50 a night, and babies stay free. A cot and high chair are there if you need them.

Outside

Kangaroos and emus feed on the property and beyond. Mornings bring the birds. In winter there are two fire pits, and after dark, the stars.

"Just the sounds of kookaburras, cockatoos and beautiful birdlife."

Gina, on Airbnb, October 2026

Fire pits and the outdoor fireplace can't be lit in fire season.
