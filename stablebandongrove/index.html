The Stable

At Bandon Grove, near Dungog

A little eco cottage for two on a working farm. It's named after its door, which opens in two.

Open the top half on a sunny morning. Keep the bottom half shut. It's the bush.

Cows and horses by day. A star‑filled sky by night.

Your own corner of an off-the-grid farm, screened from the main house 200 metres away.

One room, a loft and the view.

Nina's husband is a builder. He built The Stable for the family to live in while they built their own house, and Nina still misses its view.

  • Sleeps2, in a queen bed
  • LinenFrench linen
  • WarmthWood fire inside, fire pit outside
  • CoffeeEspresso machine, Campos pods
  • WifiNone. Telstra gets 5G
