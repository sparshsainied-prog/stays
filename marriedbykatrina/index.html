Married by Katrina

Katrina Staples

Marriage celebrant for joyful, relaxed weddings and elopements, in Nipaluna/Hobart and beyond.

Down-to-earth, and a bit of a coffee snob

Katrina's ceremonies are relaxed, heartfelt and joyful. She trained as a celebrant in 2017, and says it's an absolute privilege to stand beside you on the day.

Before that she worked in project management and office administration, and spent years as a volunteer telephone crisis supporter and shift supervisor for Lifeline. She puts those organisation and communication skills to work on your paperwork and your ceremony, so the day runs smoothly and you can relax.

  • Fur babies in your ceremony? She says yes please.
  • When you catch up, expect a great coffee, and it's her shout.
  • Off duty: yoga, bushwalking, and Tasmania's landscapes.

Two ways to do it

Both are tailored to the things that matter to you, and to your budget. Prices as on Katrina's current site.

No Fuss

$540

Up to 10 guests in total, witnesses included

Elope, or get married without the fuss. Just the two of you and your two witnesses, or a few guests as well. You'll say the words the law needs, and you can add personal vows and rings if you like.

Relaxed

$780

Up to about 30 guests

A warm, personal ceremony. The words the law needs, a bit of your story, a favourite poem or reading, your "I do", and personal vows and rings if you wish.

Katrina's fee includes

  • Unlimited contact by phone, text and email
  • A first meeting, in person or by Zoom or FaceTime, to plan your ceremony and do your legal paperwork
  • Her wedding information pack, and help with vows, readings, poems and music
  • Writing your ceremony with you
  • A catch-up a few days before, at your venue if it works, with a short rehearsal
  • Her Bose PA and Sennheiser microphone, and a signing table if you need one
  • Arriving 30 minutes early on the day, and working with your photographer and venue
  • Your presentation marriage certificate, and lodging everything with Births, Deaths and Marriages

Travel is $0.50 a kilometre, for meetings, rehearsal and the day. Your first meeting is free.

A $200 non-refundable booking fee holds your date. The balance is due 14 days before your ceremony.

She takes one booking a day, and is now taking bookings to April 2027.

Getting started

  1. Check your date

    Call Katrina or send the form below.

  2. Catch up

    In person, or by Zoom or FaceTime. Talk through your wedding and the ceremony you want.

  3. Pay your booking fee

    This secures your date and time.

  4. Do your Notice of Intended Marriage

    Bring your birth certificate and licence, or your passport. Interstate? It can be done by video.

  5. Relax

    Katrina writes the ceremony with you and guides you through each step.
