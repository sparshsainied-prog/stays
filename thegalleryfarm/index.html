The Regent Cottage at The Gallery Farm

The original farmhouse, from the early 1900s, restored by Brett for two adults. A red cedar barrel sauna and a plunge pool outside, and Brahman cattle in the paddocks.

On arrival: Denise's sourdough, farm-fresh eggs when the hens are laying, and something sweet.

Restored by a kitchen designer

Brett has spent 15 years designing kitchens, bathrooms and joinery, and won HIA Kitchen Design of the Year. The cottage got the same treatment. It's for two adults, with its own driveway and entrance.

The sauna and the pool are yours

Only cottage guests use them. The plunge pool looks over the farm, and the barrel sauna sits beside it in red cedar.

  • Fire pit down by the dam
  • Swim in the dam, or the freshwater creek when it's flowing
  • Walks around the farm and Collombatti State Forest

A working farm, 82 acres

Denise and Brett breed Brahman cattle here. The chooks come out after 10am, and dogs are welcome if you keep an eye on them around the calves.

Getting here

Don't follow your GPS. It can send you into the wrong part of the State Forest. Denise and Brett send a map once you've booked.

  1. 25 minto Kempsey for supplies. The L-Bo butcher on Elbow Street is their pick.
  2. 14 kmof graded dirt road at the end. Take it slow after rain.
  3. Minutesto Collombatti Lookout, with views from Crescent Head to Sugarloaf Mountain.
  4. CoastHat Head, Crescent Head, South West Rocks, Grassy Head and Point Plomer, a short drive away.

Good to know

  • Two adults (18+) only. Not suited to children or infants.
  • Dogs welcome. Vaccinated, tick and flea treated, off the couch and bed.
  • Mobile coverage is patchy. Starlink wifi is fast and unlimited.
  • Bring supplies. Town is 25 to 30 minutes away.
  • Non-smoking, and no parties.
  • If you sleep out in a swag, stay inside the fence. The cattle roam.
