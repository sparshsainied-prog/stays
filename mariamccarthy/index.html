Her parents: nearly 80 years married

"I am from a large family where my parents have been married for nearly 80 years." That commitment, through good times and bad, is why Maria became a celebrant.

She married late herself, after twelve years with her partner, and planning that wedding is what led her to train. She brings that life experience, as a single person and as a married woman, to every couple she works with.

Before celebrancy, Maria ran her own architectural lighting company and worked in film and television production.

From your first message to the signing

1

First contact

We talk through what you need to marry in Australia: paperwork, supporting documents, your venue, your date and my availability. Then we set a day to meet.

2

First meet up

We get to know each other and see if we are a fit. This happens one to 18 months before the wedding. If you are happy to go ahead, we complete your Notice of Intended Marriage together.

3

Next steps

A deposit secures the work on your ceremony. We stay in touch while we shape the service: vows, rituals and readings, with samples to choose from.

4

Closer to the day

The last forms are done as close to the wedding as possible. It is also a good time for a rehearsal, if you want one.

5

The wedding day

Maria usually arrives about an hour early, meets your speakers and checks everything is on plan. Afterwards she posts your paperwork and Certificate of Marriage to Births, Deaths and Marriages.

Every wedding includes

  • A ceremony written for the two of you
  • Sample vows, rituals and readings to choose from
  • All the legal paperwork, lodged for you
  • A bound copy of your ceremony to keep

Maria also leads

  • Naming days
  • Milestone celebrations
  • Funerals and memorials
