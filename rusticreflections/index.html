46 Anderson Road, Cobdogla, in the Riverland

A tiny home on a working vineyard.

For two, with ever changing views over the vines and the wetlands. Barmera is seven minutes down the road.

The vines run right up to the deck.

Big sliding glass doors open the whole vineyard side of the room.

The tiny home

One open room with a king bed, a lounge and a kitchen, and the vineyard filling the glass. Rustic charm, with the modern things you'd want: a dishwasher, a coffee machine, a smart TV and wifi.
