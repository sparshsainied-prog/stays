Little Norma

Normanville, on the Fleurieu Peninsula

A colourful 1980s house for four, a five-minute stroll from Normanville Beach.

Bed 2 Bed 1 Bath Lounge Daybed Kitchen

She's hexagon‑shaped.

Six rooms round the middle, laid out like Chelsea's own floor plan (simplified here). Two bedrooms, a bath, a daybed, a lounge and the kitchen.

1980s bones, reimagined inside.

Art by local artists, homewares from local makers, and vintage furniture. Beach and picnic gear is there for you to use, and the cupboards come stocked.

  • 4guests, in a queen and two king singles
  • +1on the daybed, linen on request
  • 2showers, one of them outdoors
  • 3dogs at most. Pets are welcome

"Our favourite room in the house. We think you'll agree."

Bring the dog.

Little Norma is pet friendly, with a fully fenced private backyard, a deck and a warm outdoor shower in the garden. Up to three pets.

Five minutes to the sand.

Down the street to Normanville Beach and the jetty, or the other way to the cafes, shops and the pub on Main Road. Chelsea drew the map.
