The Wayfarer

A 1970s Tassie beach shack above Pirates Bay, Eaglehawk Neck.

Check dates See inside

Fall asleep to the lullaby of the waves.

The Wayfarer is a classic 1970s shack, lovingly restored, with big windows over Pirates Bay and the bush all round. Two bedrooms, a Nectre wood fire, and a wrap-around deck that catches the morning sun.

It suits couples, families, friends or a solo trip, and it's an easy base for Port Arthur and the Three Capes walk.

Binoculars at arm's reach.

Guests find a pair in the shack, with a bird guide. If you're lucky, you might spot

a migrating whale, a playful pod of dolphins, a passing yacht, or even an adventurous hang glider. And on the right night, an aurora.

The list comes from the shack's own description. The aurora photo is from their listing.

Simple touches and family treasures.

Sleeps four. A queen room, a room with two singles, a new bathroom, a sun-filled sitting room and a full kitchen. Basic breakfast provisions are left for your first morning.

Getting here

  • 500 mwalk to the surf beach at Pirates Bay
  • 20 mindrive to Port Arthur
  • 50 mindrive to Hobart Airport
  • 10 minto the small grocery store at Murdunna

There are no big shops close by, so stop in Sorell for groceries on the way. The driveway is a little steep at the road, recently concreted and sealed, with a turning circle at the top.
