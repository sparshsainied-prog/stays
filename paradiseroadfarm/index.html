Claude Road, Sheffield, North West Tasmania

Two cabins with Mount Roland in the window.

A 100 acre beef farm at the turn off to Paradise, on the road to Cradle Mountain. Breakfast from the farm and local growers is in the rate.

The dam, the creek and Mount Roland.

Platypus live in the dams and creek, and ours are pretty relaxed about people.

The cabins

Two studio cabins, built for two. The bed sits up on a raised platform, so by day you look at Mount Roland and the paddocks, and by night at the milky way and the southern horizon.

If you're lucky, you can see an aurora without getting out of bed.

After dark

From the raised bed you look out at the milky way and the southern horizon. At night the farm comes alive with possums and wallabies.

The two cabins under the milky way.

Breakfast is in the rate

Freshly baked sourdough, homemade granola with Tasmanian hazelnuts, Tasmanian dairy, local honey and jam made with berries from the garden. You pick what you'd like after you book.

In the warmer months you can buy a steak from the farm shop and cook it on the Weber Q in the old shearing shed, keeping an eye on the dam for platypus while it cooks.

Sheffield's cafes and the pub are five minutes' drive away.

The farm

A working beef farm on the old Home Leigh dairy. Speckle Park cattle move from paddock to paddock, so some weeks you won't see them and other weeks they're right outside your window. We don't usually cut hay, so it can look a bit wild.

Book with us directly, and $25 goes into the ground.

Every direct booking puts at least $25 into our regenerative farm fund. Guests' money has paid for the fencing that keeps the cattle and goats off a new shelter bed along the south west boundary, planted with Mount Roland Land Care.
