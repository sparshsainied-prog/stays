Acorn Nook

A tiny house under the oaks at Lazy Ballerina winery, on the edge of Kuitpo Forest.

Check dates on Airbnb See inside

Room for two, a clawfoot bath outside, and a cellar door a stone's throw away.

The Nook is built for two and styled on wabi-sabi (less is more) principles. It runs on a trickle of solar power: enough for the fridge, the TV and your phones, though not a hair dryer. There's wifi the whole time.

James Douglas and his wife Miriam own Lazy Ballerina. James is a winemaker who grew up in Willunga. Guests say the two of them are generous with tips on other wineries and places to eat.

Inside the Nook

A queen bed in the bedroom and another in the living room, a kitchenette with a barbecue, an indoor shower, a TV and air conditioning. Rustic timber, a stone basin and pierced screens let the light through.

The bath is outside

A clawfoot tub on the deck, tucked under the oak trees. Three of the five newest reviews bring it up.

"What's better than an outdoor bath? Nothing, that's what."Jessica, Airbnb

The winery is next door

The Nook sits behind the Lazy Ballerina cellar door. On weekends you can join other guests in the garden, or keep to yourselves on the deck. Search Acorn Nook rather than Lazy Ballerina in your maps, as one guest suggests.

On the brink of Kuitpo Forest

  • Next doorKuitpo Forest: three Native Forest Reserves, 469 hectares of bush and birdlife
  • 10 minMcLaren Vale, Willunga, Mount Compass and Meadows
  • A drivePort Willunga, Maslins, Aldinga and Silver Sands beaches, and the Adelaide Hills

The Willunga Markets and the local wedding venues are close by too.
