The cottage

Built around 1930 and fully renovated, the cottage stands on its own, apart from the family's house. It runs off the grid, with an open fire inside and a spa bath outside.

"Our kids (9 and 6) loved every minute there, running on the lawn, feeding the sheep and chatting to the chooks."

The farm

120 acres, run as a family regenerative farm. The family are first-generation farmers who left the city for a simpler life, to bring the land back and grow good food along the way. Greg has walked guests round it and answered their questions on regenerative farming.
